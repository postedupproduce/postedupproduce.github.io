how it works

  1. Order by 9 PM Friday. You pay up front, by card or Apple Pay.
  2. Saturday morning we hand-pick it at the farmers market, from farms and makers within a close radius.
  3. Pickup in Goose Island (12–1), Hyde Park (2–3) or Rogers Park (4–5), or we deliver.

Expecting? Write "expecting" in Notes and any cheese or cider we pick for you will be pasteurized.
