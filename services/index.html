Sourcing services

Market food for your table, whatever the table.

Not every week is a market box week. Hosting a gathering, feeding a team, stocking a retreat kitchen or a restaurant line? Tell us the headcount and the vibe. We shop small farms and local makers at the Chicago farmers market and bring it to you.

  • Hand-picked the morning of, from a close radius
  • Ready to set out, or raw for your kitchen
  • One text thread from quote to drop-off

Starting points

Pick one as a base and we tune it to your group. Every quote is built from what's at market that week.

  • Market Snack DropPer personSeasonal fruit, fresh bread, local cheese and cider, set-out ready. For classes, meetings and break rooms. One-time or weekly.
  • Gathering Board10 · 25 · 50A bread, cheese, preserves and fruit spread sized to your headcount. Add local flowers for the table.
  • Team Market DayPer personA guided Saturday walk through the market with tastings. Everyone builds a take-home box. Group minimum.
  • Retreat KitchenPer guestMenu-planned produce, grains, eggs and bread for the whole stay, plus grab-and-go snacks between sessions.
  • Kitchen Standing OrderWeeklySend your list. We pull it from several farms and makers and drop it as one order, on one bill.
  • Holiday Gift BoxPer boxShelf-stable local goods for clients and teams: preserves, syrup, grains, hot sauce. Give us 3 to 4 weeks for big orders.

How it works

  1. Tell us the event. Date, headcount, budget and anything to avoid. Takes a minute below.
  2. Get a quote by text. We suggest what's in season and price it out with you.
  3. Pay up front. Card, Apple Pay or Zelle, by 9 PM the night before market day.
  4. We shop and drop. Picked at market that morning and brought to your door, venue or kitchen.

We shop Saturday markets right now, so the more notice the better. A week out is ideal for bigger groups.
