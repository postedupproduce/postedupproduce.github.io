What it is

Your personal shopper at the farmers market.

We shop small farms and makers at the Chicago farmers market, pick the best of the week for you, and hand it off the same day at a meet-up near you.

No subscription. No season commitment. Order the weeks you want.

Why it's different

  • Grocery storeFarm → packer → distributor → warehouse → store → you. Farmers get about 18¢ of every grocery food dollar (USDA, 2024).
  • Posted UpFarm stand → your shopper → you.

Started by a South Side organizer with 20+ years in Chicago food systems, to get more people eating from local farms without the 7 AM market run.

We pay the farmer their full stand price, so every dollar we spend on your food goes to the farm or maker who grew or baked it. Our shopping and handoff fee is built into our prices.

How it works

  1. Order and pay by 9 PM the night before, by card, Apple Pay, Zelle or Apple Cash. Pick a box or build your own.
  2. We shop the morning market and hand-select what's peaking that week.
  3. Pick it up or get it delivered the same day.
