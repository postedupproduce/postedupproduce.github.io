Why it's different

  • Grocery storeFarm → packer → distributor → warehouse → store → you. Days in cold storage along the way. Farmers get about 18¢ of every grocery food dollar (USDA, 2024).
  • Posted UpFarm stand → your shopper → you. Hours, in a cooler we check by hand.

Run by a Chicagoan with 20+ years in local food: farmers markets, urban farms and community food programs, Produce Safety Alliance certified. Built so more people eat from local farms without the 7 AM market run.

We pay the farmer their full stand price, so every dollar we spend on your food goes to the farm or maker who grew or baked it. Our shopping and handoff fee is built into our prices.

Every farm we buy from is certified. The market we shop was the first in the country to require every farmer to hold a third-party sustainability certification: USDA Organic, Certified Naturally Grown, Food Alliance, Animal Welfare Approved and others. We only say "organic" when a farm is certified organic, and neither can they. Ask about any item and we'll tell you the farm's certification.

Picked that morning. Hours to your hands, not days in a warehouse. Hand-picked, or it's on us: not happy with an item, or it wasn't at market? Credit or refund, your choice.
