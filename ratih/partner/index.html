step by step

what to do

  1. Pick the recipe and the send date in the form at the bottom. We'll turn that recipe into the kit on your readers' page within a day.
  2. Look over your readers' page and tell us anything you'd change: words, the offer, the look.
  3. Put the link in your post. Use the newsletter link below. If you share it on Instagram too, use the Instagram link so we can tell you which one brought people in.
  4. That's it. After it goes out, we'll send you how many readers visited and ordered.
