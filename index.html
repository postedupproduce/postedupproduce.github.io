    Order Terms

    Version: October 2026. By paying for an order, you agree to these terms.

    1. What we do. Posted Up Produce is a personal shopping service. You pay us to shop farmers markets and local makers for you and hand off your order. We don't grow or make the food ourselves.
    2. Payment. Pay in full by 9 PM the night before pickup. Your order is confirmed only when payment arrives and we text you a confirmation. Unpaid orders are not shopped.
    3. What you get. You get the box or item groups you ordered, chosen by us at market that day. Examples on the menu are typical, not promised. Weights are approximate.
    4. Items we can't get. If something you paid for isn't available, its full price becomes a credit on your next order. Credits are used once, on your next order. Want cash back instead? Just ask and we refund it within 2 days, the same way you paid. Any credit not used by the end of the pilot (November 21) is refunded automatically. If you opted in to swaps, we may substitute an item of equal or greater value instead, at no extra charge. We tell you what changed before or at handoff.
    5. Cancellations. Cancel by 9 PM the night before pickup for a full refund. After that, we've committed to shop for you, so we can't refund a cancellation.
    6. Pickup and delivery. Pick up during your chosen window. Orders not picked up by the end of the window aren't refunded, but text us and we'll try to arrange a late handoff or delivery for the delivery fee. Delivery fees are confirmed by text before you pay.
    7. Quality. If something arrives damaged or spoiled, text us a photo within 24 hours. We refund or replace that item.
    8. Food safety. Wash produce before eating. Keep frozen items frozen; they're your responsibility once handed off. Baked goods may contain wheat, dairy, eggs, nuts or other allergens and may be made in shared kitchens. Tell us about allergies in your order notes.
    9. Limit. Our responsibility for any order is limited to the amount you paid for it.
    10. Questions. Text 773-807-9223 or email cultivate.regenerate@gmail.com.

    Fresh produce should always be washed before eating, even when farm-direct. Availability depends on weather, harvest and which makers are at market that day.
