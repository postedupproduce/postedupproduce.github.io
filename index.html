          Order Terms

          Version: October 2026. By paying for an order, you agree to these terms.

          1. What we do. Posted Up Produce is a personal shopping service operated by 123 Footprint, LLC ("we"). You pay us to shop farmers markets and local makers for you and hand off your order. We don't grow or make the food ourselves.
          2. Payment. Pay in full by 9 PM the night before pickup, by Zelle or Apple Cash. Your order is confirmed only when payment arrives and we text you a confirmation. Unpaid orders are not shopped. We can't take cash or card for orders at pickup, because we buy everything at market that morning. Cash is fine at pickup for tips or a small extra you add by text.
          3. What you get. You get the box or item groups you ordered, chosen by us at market that day. Examples on the menu are typical, not promised. Weights are approximate.
          4. Items we can't get. If something you paid for isn't available, its full price becomes a credit on your next order. Credits are used once, on your next order. Want cash back instead? Just ask and we refund it within 2 days, the same way you paid. Any credit not used by the end of the pilot (November 21) is refunded automatically. If you opted in to swaps, we may substitute an item of equal or greater value instead, at no extra charge. We tell you what changed before or at handoff.
          5. Cancellations. Cancel by 9 PM the night before pickup for a full refund. After that, we've committed to shop for you, so we can't refund a cancellation.
          6. Pickup days. Pickups are on the day we shop: Saturdays at Hyde Park (3–4 PM) through November 21, the last box of the pilot. Delivery is same-day. Other days or times may be arranged by text.
          7. Beta founder rate. Boxes (produce, bread, Meat Lovers Box and Lake Haul) picked up at a meet-up during the pilot are 10% off; delivery orders and add-ons aren't included. Customers who complete at least one paid meet-up order during the pilot become Founding Members and keep 10% off boxes through the 2027 season, if Posted Up continues operating. It can't be combined with other box promotions we offer later, and it doesn't stack with free Standing Order delivery: meet up and save 10%, or take free delivery, each week.
          8. Free early drop-off. On pickup orders you can opt in to a free early drop-off at your door. It's never guaranteed: we text you first when our route allows it. If we don't text, pick up at your meet-up window as usual. Questions: text 773-807-9223.
          9. Standing Order. A Standing Order is a weekly order we set up by text. Each week we send a pay link; your box is shopped only once it's paid by 9 PM the night before. Skip any week by 9 PM the night before, or stop anytime, by text. Unpaid by the cutoff means no box and no charge. Standing Order delivery is free within 3 miles of the meet-up stop; beyond that we text you the per-mile add before you pay. Every 4th paid Standing Order box includes a free add-on (pastry, hot sauce or cider) of our choosing from what's at market.
          10. Cooler bag deposit. If we pack your order in a Posted Up cooler bag, we add a $15 deposit to that order. Hand the bag back at any meet-up or delivery and the full $15 becomes a credit on your next order.
          11. Feedback thank-you. Leave feedback with your contact info during the beta and we'll add one bonus produce pick to your next order. One per person.
          12. Photo thank-you. Send a photo of your Posted Up order with the form on this page and we’ll add one free pastry or small-batch hot sauce to your next paid order. One per person during the beta. By sending a photo you confirm you took it and let us share it on our site and social pages, credited to you if you give a handle.
          13. Pickup and delivery. Pick up during your chosen window. Orders not picked up by the end of the window aren't refunded, but text us and we'll try to arrange a late handoff or delivery for the delivery fee. Delivery fees are confirmed by text before you pay.
          14. Quality. If something arrives damaged or spoiled, text us a photo within 24 hours. We refund or replace that item.
          15. Food safety. Wash produce before eating. Keep frozen items frozen; they're your responsibility once handed off. Baked goods may contain wheat, dairy, eggs, nuts or other allergens and may be made in shared kitchens. Tell us about allergies in your order notes.
          16. Privacy. We count site visits anonymously to see what's working: no cookies, no names, no IP addresses. Order and form details are used only to fill and follow up on your order or message.
          17. Limit. Our responsibility for any order is limited to the amount you paid for it.
          18. Questions. Text 773-807-9223 or use the feedback form on this page.
