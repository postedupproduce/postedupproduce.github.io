How to play
  1. Each track is one thing in your box. The green card is what's planned. Tap another card to swap it.
  2. Want something else? Tap Wild card and pick a swap from the list.
  3. Tap Lock it in to move to the next track. The crowd meter fills as you go, and Back lets you change your mind.
  4. Encore: tell me what you'd love to see, add the extra if you want it, and pick your show date and venue (pickup day and meet-up spot).
  5. Print the setlist, tap Copy my setlist, and text it to 773-807-9223. I'll confirm and send payment details.

Your picks stay on your device until you text them. Want your own version? Tap Make your own at the top.

Make your own box

Name your box, set the tracks and swaps, then copy the box code and text it to your crew. They open this page, tap Make your own, and paste the code. Changes show up in the game right away. Prices are set by Posted Up for real orders; we confirm every custom box by text before you pay.
