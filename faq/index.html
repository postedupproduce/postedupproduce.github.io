The basics

What is Posted Up?

A personal shopper for the Chicago farmers market. You order a box, we shop small farms and makers at the market Saturday morning, pick the best of the week, and hand it to you that afternoon at a meet-up near you, or deliver it. We don't grow or make the food; we shop it for you.

How does it work, step by step?

1. Order and pay by Friday 9 PM. 2. We shop the market at sunrise Saturday. 3. Pick it up at a meet-up (Goose Island 12–1, Hyde Park 2–3, Rogers Park 4–5) or get it delivered the same day.

Is it a subscription?

No. Order the weeks you want, skip the weeks you don't. If you want the same box every Saturday, a Standing Order sets that up by text: we send a pay link each week, you skip any week by Friday 9 PM, and you can stop anytime.

Who's behind it?

Posted Up Produce is a beta run by 123 Footprint, LLC, run by a Chicagoan with 20+ years in local food: farmers markets, urban farms and community food programs, Produce Safety Alliance certified. Questions go to a real person: email info@postedupproduce.org.

Money

How much does it cost?

Half Peck $50, Full Crate $72, The Vegan Box $68, Meat Lovers Box $89, Lake Haul $70, Better Bites $35. Add-ons like bread, eggs and cheese are priced per item. Boxes picked up at a meet-up during the beta are 10% off (the founder rate). Everything we spend on food goes to the farm or maker at their full stand price; our shopping and handoff fee is built into the box price.

How do I pay?

By card, Apple Pay or Zelle, in full by Friday 9 PM. Your order is shopped only once it's paid; we text a confirmation. We can't take cash or card for orders at pickup, because we buy everything at market that morning. Cash at pickup is fine for a tip or a small extra you add by text.

Can I cancel?

Yes, by Friday 9 PM for a full refund. After that we've committed to shop for you, so a cancellation can't be refunded.

Are there discounts?

Two. The beta founder rate: 10% off boxes picked up at a meet-up, and if you complete one paid meet-up order during the pilot you keep that 10% through the 2027 season. The Seasoned Rate: 60 or older, 10% off your whole order, delivery included, on the honor system; tick the box at checkout. They stack.

Pickup and delivery

Where and when do I pick up?

Saturdays, the same day we shop: Goose Island 12–1 PM, Hyde Park 2–3 PM, Rogers Park 4–5 PM, through Nov 21, the last outdoor market. We text you when we're posted up. Other days or times can be arranged by text.

Do you deliver? What does it cost?

Yes, same day: after 1 PM near Goose Island, after 3 PM near Hyde Park, after 5 PM near Rogers Park. $8 within 3 miles of a meet-up stop. Farther out, text us your address and we quote it before you pay. Standing Order delivery is free within 3 miles.

What if I miss my pickup window?

Text us. Same day, we'll meet you at a later stop or hold your box until the last one. Another day costs the delivery fee, since we're making a trip. Your box is packed cold either way.

Can I order for someone else?

Yes. Put their name and phone number in the Notes at checkout and we'll text them the pickup or delivery details. Good for a new parent, a friend who's sick, or a thank-you.

The food

What's in the box?

Whatever is peaking that week, chosen by us at the market. The examples on the menu are typical, not promised, and weights are approximate. Right now that means apples, pears, sweet potatoes, beets, squash, greens, onions and garlic, with bread, eggs, cheese, meat and fish as add-ons.

Can I say what I want?

Yes. Pick Shopper's Choice and tell us what you love or never want in the Notes, or Build Your Own and choose the kinds of things you want; we buy the best example at market. Vegan, vegetarian, pescatarian and no-pork boxes are on the menu.

What about allergies?

Your diet rides along on your order so we shop for it. We pack in a shared space, not an allergen-free kitchen. For a serious allergy, text us before you order and we'll tell you honestly whether we can do it.

Is it organic?

Some of it is certified organic, and we say so only when the farm holds that certification. All of it is certified something: the market we shop was the first in the country to require every farmer to carry a third-party sustainability certification (USDA Organic, Certified Naturally Grown, Food Alliance, Animal Welfare Approved and others), and farms there can't use the word "organic" without the USDA certificate. Ask about any item and we'll tell you which certification its farm holds.

Something was missing, or not good?

Hand-picked, or it's on us. If an item you paid for wasn't at market, its full price becomes a credit on your next order, or ask and we refund it within 2 days the way you paid. Not happy with something? Same: credit or refund, your choice. If you opted in to swaps, we may substitute something of equal or greater value and tell you at handoff.

How is it kept cold?

Hours from the stand to your hands, in a cooler we check by hand. If your order rides in a Posted Up cooler bag, there's a $15 deposit you get back when you return it.
