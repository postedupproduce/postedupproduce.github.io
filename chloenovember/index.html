HOW IT WORKS
  1. Build your list here and send it by Thursday night.
  2. Friday we confirm what's at the farmers market and send one quote.
  3. Pay up front by 9 PM Friday with card or Apple Pay. We front the first $100 of the shopping, so you prepay the rest and settle up to $100 when we drop.
  4. Saturday we shop at opening and drop it at your door. Pickup or delivery; delivery is $8 within 3 miles of our Goose Island stop, and we confirm yours.
