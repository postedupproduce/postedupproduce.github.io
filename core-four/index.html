Questions.

Why just one Saturday?

Dec 5 is the first indoor farmers market of the winter. We're starting small, 5 boxes, to get it right. If you love it, tell us and it comes back.

Why don't I pick the items?

In winter, nobody knows which farms will be at the farmers market until the doors open. So we promise the four groups and pick the best of each that morning.

What if the farmers market is out of something?

We swap within the same group. You always get all four.

How do I know what's in my box?

We text you the exact list and your recipe card Saturday morning, before pickup.

What's a "plate"?

One dinner-size serving, made from the box plus pantry basics like oil, salt and a grain. Plates per box are our estimate and depend on how you cook.

Plant-based or vegetarian?

Every box is vegetarian. Plant-based? Add a note when you order: we swap tofu for eggs and pick a plant-based fat when the farmers market has one.

Allergies or things I won't eat?

Add a note when you order. We skip them and pick something else from the same group.

How long does it keep?

Squash, roots and apples keep for weeks somewhere cool. Eat greens first, within about 5 days. Each recipe card says what to cook first.

What if something's not right?

Hand-picked, or it's on us. Not happy with an item? Credit or refund, your choice.
