A NOD TO REVEL & WELL

Built on the Core Four.

The Core Four comes from Revel & Well, Monique Mercurio's nutrition coaching practice: build every meal on protein, healthy fat, fiber and colorful vegetables. We loved it, so we built a farmers market box around it.

Visit Revel & Well

Questions.

Why don't I pick the items?

In winter, nobody knows which farms will be at the farmers market until the doors open. So we promise the four groups and pick the best of each that morning. Want to choose every item? Build your own from our à la carte menu.

What's different about No-Cook?

Same four groups, but every item is ready to eat or just needs slicing: tofu, cheese, bread, fruit, greens and ferments instead of raw eggs and roots. It costs a little more because ready-to-eat food costs more at the stand.

What if the farmers market is out of something?

We swap within the same group. You always get all four.

How do I know what's in my box?

We text you the exact list and your recipe cards Saturday morning, before pickup.

What's a "plate"?

One dinner-size serving, made from the box plus pantry basics like oil, salt and a grain. Plates per box are our estimate and depend on how you cook.

Plant-based or vegetarian?

Vegetarian is easy: eggs, cheese and butter are all vegetarian. Plant-based? Tell us when you order. We swap tofu for eggs and pick a plant-based fat when the farmers market has one.

Allergies or things I won't eat?

Put them in your order notes. We skip them and pick something else from the same group.

How long does it keep?

Roots, squash and apples keep for weeks somewhere cool. Eat greens and mushrooms first, within about 5 days. Each recipe card says what to cook first.

Can I add things?

Yes: bread, a pastry, cider or hot sauce at checkout.

Can I skip a week?

On a standing order, reply SKIP or tell us by Friday 9 PM. You only pay for the weeks you get a box.

What if something's not right?

Hand-picked, or it's on us. Not happy with an item? Credit or refund, your choice.
