Roasted delicata

  1. Heat the oven to 425.
  2. Cut the squash in half and scoop out the seeds.
  3. Slice into half-moons. Leave the skin on, you eat it.
  4. Toss on a sheet pan with oil and salt.
  5. Roast 25 minutes, flip once halfway.

Serves two. Keeps 4 days in the fridge.
